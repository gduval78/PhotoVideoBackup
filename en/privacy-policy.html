Privacy policy — PhotoVideoBackup

Last updated: 2026-10-22

PhotoVideoBackup does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own.

What the app accesses

  • Your photos and videos — read-only, to copy them to the drive, folder or NAS that you choose. On Android this includes the location stored in your photos, so that the copy is identical to the original.
  • The folders and cards that you pick with the system file browser.
  • Notifications, to tell you when a backup ends.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing goes to us. Your files are written only to the destinations you choose.
  • If you configure a NAS, the app connects to that server, at the address you typed, and nowhere else. The password is kept in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore.
  • iOS or Android may download your own iCloud or Google photos so the app can read them; this is done by the system, for your account.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. We receive no payment information.

Diagnostic log

The app keeps a small diagnostic log on your device, with no personal content. It is sent only if you choose to e-mail it to support from the app.

Contact with support

If you write to support, we receive what you send — your address and your message — and use it only to answer you.

Support: photovideobackup@icloud.com
